Your Collection

Your checkboxes are stored locally in your browser on the device you use. The My Collection page gives you an overview and lets you export your progress as a JSON file.

Tip: if you change phones or clear browser storage, your locally saved checklist may not carry over, so use Export My Collection when you want a backup.
